This app overlays NAR mailing postal codes and mailing municipality
names onto the GeoNames directory so newer local delivery units
(for example the V8H codes for Cobble Hill, Mill Bay, and Shawnigan
Lake) resolve to the correct community while offline.

Adapted from the July 2025 NAR extract of postal code to preferred
city name.
